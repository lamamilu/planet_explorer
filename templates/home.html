Notice!

The orbit is almost always not a perfect circle but rather an ellipse. This causes the planet's distance from the star to constantly change.

The planet slows down as it gets further away from the star and speeds up as it gets closer. This happens because the gravity of the star's pull on the planet gets stronger the closer they are.
